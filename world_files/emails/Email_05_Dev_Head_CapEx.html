Toured the property yesterday. Here's my take:

The lobby needs a full renovation - not the "soft refurb" the broker's describing. I'd budget around $85 per square foot for the entrance and lobby area. Bathrooms on floors 1 through 5 need a complete redo, figure $100 per square foot. Corridors on floors 1 through 5 could use a refresh at about $35 per square foot, but the upper floors are fine.

For the spec suites on the Meridian space, $65 per square foot gets you a Class A finish - glass fronts, open plan, kitchen, phone rooms. That's a big chunk of space to build out across three floors.

I think 14 to 16 months for the lobby and common areas, and the spec suites can run in parallel starting a couple months after close but they'll need to be phased floor by floor. Rest of the common areas are in acceptable shape.
